# Code of conduct

## The short version

This project exists to help people who are routinely ignored by institutions. Behave accordingly.

## Our standards

**Expected:**

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements here are about facts, and facts can be checked.
- Argue with the claim, not the person.
- Say "I don't know" when you don't. This project has more open questions than answers, and pretending
  otherwise produces wrong civic data.
- Cite sources. "I read somewhere that the DLP is 5 years" is not a contribution; a link to the
  contract clause is.
- Respect that contributors are in different time zones, have day jobs, and speak different first
  languages. Slow replies are not disrespect.
- Be careful with real citizen data. Never paste it into an issue, a PR, or a chat.

**Not acceptable:**

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind
- Discrimination or derogatory comments on the basis of caste, religion, region, language, gender,
  sexuality, disability, or any other characteristic
- Political-party advocacy or partisan framing in project spaces
- Publishing anyone's private information
- Sustained disruption of discussions
- Sexualised language or imagery
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern

## Project-specific expectations

Because of what this project does:

| Expectation | Why |
|---|---|
| **Do not use project data to target an individual** | The platform names contracts and designations, not people's private lives |
| **Do not use the project for political campaigning** | Neutrality is the platform's most fragile and most necessary asset |
| **Do not overstate what the data shows** | An overclaimed finding damages every future finding |
| **Take the naming of private firms seriously** | Real businesses and real people are affected. Sourced facts only |
| **Escalate legal questions rather than guessing** | We are engineers |

## Scope

Applies in all project spaces — repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, chat, events — and
when representing the project publicly.

## Reporting

Report concerns to `conduct@tracesarkar.org` *(to be provisioned)*. Reports are handled
confidentially. If your concern involves a maintainer, say so; we will arrange for someone else to
handle it.

We will:
1. Acknowledge within 3 working days
2. Investigate fairly, hearing from everyone involved
3. Decide and communicate the outcome
4. Keep the reporter's identity confidential to the extent possible

## Enforcement

| Severity | Response |
|---|---|
| Minor / first instance | Private correction, with the specific behaviour named |
| Repeated or moderate | Public warning; temporary restriction from project spaces |
| Serious | Temporary ban |
| Severe or repeated after a ban | Permanent ban |

Enforcement decisions can be appealed once, in writing, to be reviewed by someone not involved in the
original decision.
