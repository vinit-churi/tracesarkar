TraceSarkar · Mumbai Methodology

Methodology

Where the facts on this platform come from, how each one is measured, and — at least as important — what it does not know.

As of 6 October 2026. Every figure below was measured, not estimated.

1 · What this answers

A resident photographs a civic problem. The platform resolves which authority is responsible for that spot, which of its departments owns that kind of problem, what obliges them and by when, and — where the data exists — which public contract covers the stretch and who holds it.

The join a point and its GPS accuracy → the ward polygon that contains it → the road segments within a search radius derived from that accuracy → the contract those segments belong to → the contractor named in it, with the document it was read from and the time it was read.

It states facts and the joins between them. It does not conclude that anyone has done anything wrong.

2 · Where the data comes from

WhatSourceHow
Road works, contractors, dates, geometry BMC's own roads API Collected twice daily; every response archived before it is parsed
Ward boundaries BMC's own boundary layer The same API the works come from, so the two agree by construction
Which department owns which problem BMC Section 4(1)(b) RTI handbooks, per ward, per department Read first-hand and archived; designations only, never named individuals
Deadlines and penalties A High Court order, a BMC road tender, a BMC RTI handbook Quoted with citation; see §4
What is in a photograph A vision model, over a fixed six-category taxonomy Structured output, versioned prompt, platform rules applied after

The archive is the record, not the parsed row. Public bodies move and delete documents without notice — BMC's own Citizen's Charter URL already returns a 404. Every source document is stored as retrieved, with a digest and a timestamp, so a claim can be checked against what was actually published rather than against our reading of it.

3 · What has been measured

98.17%
Ward resolution agreement, over 2,405 road works
BMC's works endpoint states each work's ward; BMC's boundary endpoint decides it. Two independent statements from the same API agreed on 2,361 of 2,405. The bar was ≥95%.
≥ 92.9%
Contract attribution precision, honest lower bound
Fifty of fifty answers judged right by a human. The figure shown is the 95% confidence floor for a clean run of that size — fifty out of fifty does not mean the next fifty will be. Zero wrong-contractor matches. Judged against displaced probe points, not photographs; see §5.
43–57%
Of Borivali carriageway with contract geometry
Measured against OpenStreetMap: 43% within 5 m, 57% within 25 m. Better on busier roads — secondary 77%, tertiary 69%. So "we have no contract data for this stretch" is a normal answer, not a failure.

4 · The deadlines, and who they bind

No deadline is written into the software. Each lives in a versioned table with its citation and the date it took effect.

ObligationBindsSource
48 hours to attend a pothole, once the corporation has been notified The corporation High Court of Bombay, PIL 71/2013, order of 13 October 2025, para 70(ix), 2025:BHC-OS:18736-DB
24 hours to rectify a defect, from intimation The contractor, during the defect liability period BMC road tender ETH_8000040832, clause 10.11
₹5,000 per day, per defect The contractor Same tender, clause 7.4
24 hours to remove refuse, and to remove silt and debris The corporation R/Central Solid Waste Management RTI handbook, §4(1)(b)(iii); MMC Act 1888 ss. 365(a) and 375(A)

The clocks run from notification, not from the moment a problem exists, and not from the moment it is photographed here. Photographing something on this platform notifies nobody. The complaint to the authority's own channel is what starts them.

Water supply has no published repair deadline. The R/Central Water Works handbook sets time limits for granting a connection, reading a meter and restoring a disconnected supply, and none for repairing a burst main. Where a document creates no obligation, this platform does not invent one by borrowing another.

5 · What this does not know

Limitations

  • One ward. Departments and deadlines are recorded for R/Central, Borivali. A capture elsewhere in Mumbai resolves to a ward and stops there. A capture outside Greater Mumbai resolves to nothing — it is kept, and the platform says plainly that it is outside every boundary held.
  • Three categories of six. Road defects, waste and water supply have a department behind them. The other three are recognised and have nowhere to be sent, and are told so.
  • Classification accuracy is unmeasured. There is no labelled evaluation set yet, so no accuracy figure is claimed. Anything below the confidence floor asks the person rather than guessing.
  • Attribution has been judged on probes, not photographs. The reviewer read the same map the code read, which establishes that the join picks a plausible road, not a correct one. Only photographs taken at identifiable places settle that, and they are still being collected.
  • Contract data is BMC's alone. Other corporations in the region have not been shown to publish works geometry at all.
  • Three pothole circulars are cited by BMC and published by nobody. Searched and not found; an RTI is the only route to their text.

6 · Rules this platform holds itself to

  • Nothing is ever filed automatically. Complaints, RTIs and applications are generated as drafts. A person reads one and sends it themselves. There is no code path that submits a legal instrument.
  • No unsourced fact about a named party. Every contractor name, award value or designation carries the document it came from and the time it was retrieved. Without a source, the field is not shown.
  • No assertion of wrongdoing. The platform states that a contract covers a location and that its liability period is running. It does not conclude that anyone is at fault.
  • A contract is shown only where it governs the problem. A road contract covers a stretch of road; it does not make its contractor answerable for rubbish left on it, and their name does not appear beside one.
  • Precise reporter location never reaches a public surface. Exact coordinates stay internal and visible only to the person who captured them.
  • A capture is never lost. The photograph is stored before anything is attempted on it, and every stage after that can be retried.

7 · Corrections

If a fact here is wrong — a contractor misattributed, a deadline misread, a document misquoted — it should be corrected and the correction recorded. Use the support route on this site. A named party may ask for a right of reply.

Road works, ward boundaries and department mappings are derived from documents published by the Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation. Road network comparisons use OpenStreetMap data, © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Court orders are quoted from the published judgment.
