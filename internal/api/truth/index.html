Drag a box around every face and every number plate you can see, tight to the thing itself rather than the vehicle or the person around it — including the ones at the edge of the frame, the ones out of focus, and the ones you only recognise because you were there. The detector is graded against what a person can see, so a face you leave unmarked is a face it gets away with missing.
If there is nothing to mark, say so — n. A photograph with nothing in it is the only thing that can measure a false alarm.
